## Priority order

### P0 — Confirm technical discovery
1. Confirm each page returns HTTP 200 and is reachable from the homepage through a normal HTML link.
2. Confirm each page has a self-referencing canonical URL.
3. Confirm robots.txt does not disallow the pages and there is no noindex directive.
4. Confirm every live URL appears exactly once in sitemap.xml and uses the canonical HTTPS URL.
5. Wait for sitemap processing, then re-check sitemap status and URL Inspection.

### P1 — Improve internal discovery
1. Add a clear HTML link to every service page from the homepage and relevant related-service sections.
2. Add contextual links between closely related services, without repeating keyword-stuffed anchors.
3. Keep navigation labels descriptive and useful to people.

### P2 — Improve page usefulness
1. Give each service page a distinct title, H1 and meta description.
2. Explain service scope, common problems, intervention process and service area in natural French.
3. Add real, original photos where available, with accurate alt text.
4. Make contact options and service area clear without inventing reviews, addresses, guarantees or credentials.

### P3 — Measure outcomes
1. Re-check URL Inspection after Google has had time to recrawl.
2. Re-query Search Console for page and query performance using finalized dates.
3. Track calls and WhatsApp leads separately from impressions and clicks.
4. Prioritize changes by qualified leads, not ranking position alone.

## Safety rules
- Do not generate doorway pages or mass near-duplicate city pages.
- Do not scrape Google result pages or automate search queries.
- Do not fabricate reviews, local addresses, rankings or service claims.
- Do not auto-publish content or modify live pages without a reviewable diff and backup.
